Akademia Gracia

CONCEPT

Akademia Gracia is a rehabilitation center in Lviv with 35 years of history, specializing in non-surgical correction of posture and scoliosis for children and adults. The brand had outgrown its old identity — pink, cluttered, and closer to a beauty salon than a center of medical expertise.

CHALLENGE

Balance scientific credibility with human warmth — without looking clinical or childish. The visual language needed to speak to both worried parents and adults living with chronic pain, with honesty and without fear.

SOLUTION

The identity is built around a custom monogram of the letter "A" — a thick vertical stroke for structure and posture, paired with a thin calligraphic curve for movement and grace. A warm palette of coral and teal replaces the old aggressive pink, paired with a friendly, honest tone of voice across all communication.

Module

CONCEPT

Module is a minimalist coffee spot designed for people who work from their laptop — a "third place" between home and office. A quiet, focused space where specialty coffee meets productivity, inspired by industrial aesthetics: concrete, steel, and open space that helps visitors stay in the flow.

CHALLENGE

The brand is built on a geometric grid and technical precision. A stylized rabbit mascot is integrated into the letter "M," adding personality to the strict modular system — symbolizing agility and mental focus, mirroring the people who come here to create and work.

SOLUTION

Neutral industrial grays combine with a bright Electric Blue, creating a look that's modern, energetic, and grounded at the same time. A palette built to feel both calm enough to focus in, and sharp enough to stand out.

Mescla

CONCEPT

Mescla, derived from the Italian word for "mixture," is a natural wine brand built around a strange and vivid myth: a goat once wandered into a vineyard, stripped the young vines bare — and by accident, taught winemakers the art of pruning.

The brand's mark merges human and goat into a single wild figure, "kin to the wine god" himself — leaning into the raw, untamed character of natural wine: unfiltered, unpolished, and true to instinct.

CHALLENGE

Giving a surreal, mythological figure enough restraint to still function as a wine label — not as an illustration, but as a mark that could scale across three different wine variants without losing legibility. Balancing the wild, expressive linework of the goat-human figure with a calm, editorial typography system (Fraunces) was key to keeping the brand feeling intentional rather than chaotic.

SOLUTION

The final identity uses a single fluid, hand-drawn mark across a three-bottle range — Goat Red, Goat Gold, and Goat Bone — where only the accent color shifts to reflect each wine's character. The result is a natural wine brand that feels primal and instinctive, yet remains clean enough to stand confidently on a shelf.

Iskra

CONCEPT

ISKRA is a prebiotic sparkling drink brand where health meets a bold, "acid" aesthetic. The name means "spark" — and the identity captures that energy through stylized geometric patterns and vibrant color, proving that sugar-free wellness can look as exciting as it feels.

CHALLENGE

Sugar-free and prebiotic drinks often lean soft, clinical, or purely "healthy" in their branding — ISKRA needed to break that pattern without losing trust in the product itself. The challenge was building a system punchy enough to stand out on a shelf, while staying flexible across three very different flavors.

SOLUTION

Each flavor gets its own faceted fruit illustration — strawberry-lemon, mint-pineapple, blueberry-lavender — built from sharp geometric shapes that echo the brand's spark motif. A bold color per can, a starburst mark, and confident type give ISKRA a shelf presence that feels energetic first, "healthy" second.

Rebro

CONCEPT

Rebro is a geometric grotesque — pragmatic, with a strong character. A typeface for those who want to speak direct. Its character is strict but not aggressive — more precise than harsh. Inspired by the aesthetics of industrial road signs, the clean lines of engineering drafts, early computer interfaces, and street lettering in general.

PURPOSE

Built for headlines, branding, and environments where clarity matters more than decoration — where the message needs to land without extra words getting in the way.

STATUS

Currently in progress, designed in FontLab. The uppercase letterset, numerals, and basic punctuation are complete. Lowercase letters are in development next.
